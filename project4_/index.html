Daydream Player

Daydream Player is a music player that transports the user into different daydreams to escape the stressful realities of life.
Each daydream has their own world to explore. You can navigate by pressing the following keys:
1 - Home Page 2 - Daydream 1 3 = Daydream 2 4 - Daydream 3 H/h - Help Page
You can also click the individual daydreams on the home page to enter their world and then use the number keys to switch between worlds.
